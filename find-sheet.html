Find Your Google Sheet

Step-by-step guide to locate your TSKA exam submissions data

1

Open Google Drive

Go to https://drive.google.com and sign in with your Gmail account.

2

Search for the Apps Script Project

In the search bar, search for:

AKfycbw1AFt6yDHq85ASqSm8DqiSp-wNFBAf6rOCnxkap_DZQ4ZctYy0vZU3oqG-ASwmPjYg

This is your Apps Script ID

3

Find the Linked Spreadsheet

Once you find the Apps Script project, look for a linked Google Sheet. The sheet name usually contains:

  • ✓ "TSKA" or "Exam"
  • ✓ "Submissions" or "Responses"
  • ✓ "Zenshin" or "Karate"
4

Open the Sheet

Right-click on the sheet and select Open, or simply click on it to view all your exam submissions data.

💡 Tip: The Google Sheet will have columns like:
Full Name | Membership | Belt | Exam Fee | UTR | Status | Timestamp

🔍 Can't Find It?

  • Check Shared with me section in Google Drive
  • Search for recent files (it should appear in "Recent" section)
  • Look in your My Drive for sheets containing "exam" or "TSKA"
  • Check if it's in a specific folder you created
